## Learning to Play with Aia

## MEET YOUR CREATOR

Find the large card that says **AIA** at the top. Set it in front of you. Place 11 Loyalty tokens next to it.

**LOYALTY: 11** — Lower than Anonymous User's 16. Aia is not built to absorb punishment. She is built to prevent it.

**FACTION: CLIP Resistance / Experimentalist** — Aia belongs to two factions. CLIP Resistance are creators who push the limits of AI generation — technically precise, patient, and deeply deliberate. Experimentalists are the trailblazers, always trying something new. Aia is both.

**STARTING BONUS: +1 Credit** — Aia begins the game with 1 extra Credit on top of her normal starting amount. Going first: 4 + 1 = 5 Credits. Going second: 6 + 1 = 7 Credits. This matters a lot — her best Model is expensive, and that extra Credit makes it reachable on Turn 1 when going second.

**FAVOURITE PROMPT:** *"vibrant expressionism by Aia, soft iridescent water reflections"* — +1 Quality and 1 bonus Visibility Counter. Counts as an Atmosphere subtype. Free, always available.

## WHAT MAKES AIA DIFFERENT

Aia plays slow and deep. Her goal is to build a small number of powerful, protected Creations — then use them as weapons.

Three things define her:

**1. Coherent (Low Settings)** — Her primary Model. Expensive (Play 3 + Activate 4 = 7 Credits) but generates Quality 3 Creations with 1 bonus Visibility Counter on entry, and is the only Model family in Set 001 that can have CLIP-LOCK applied to it. Going second (7 Credits + 1 bonus = 8), Aia can afford Coherent on Turn 1. Going first (4 + 1 = 5 Credits), she uses Juggernaut v9 instead (Play 2 + Activate 3 = 5) as a tempo play to avoid Creator Stress.

**2. CLIP-LOCK** — Aia's signature mechanic. Each turn she can lock one of her Coherent Creations, making it immune to opponent Glitch tokens and single-target abilities until she chooses to unlock it. The longer a Creation stays locked, the more she benefits from Positive Feedback when she releases it.

**3. Copy That! (Signature)** — Her ultimate ability. Unleash up to 3 locked Creations simultaneously, dealing Loyalty damage for each one. This is how the game ends.

---

## AIA'S PASSIVE — CLIP-LOCK MASTERY

*Once per turn, during your Main Phase, you may apply CLIP-LOCK to one of your active Coherent Creations. CLIP-LOCK does not apply automatically — you choose when to lock each Creation.*

**What CLIP-LOCK does:**
- The locked Creation cannot be targeted by opponent single-target abilities
- The locked Creation cannot receive opponent Glitch tokens
- The Creation is not destroyed when its Quality would reach 0 from opponent Glitch effects (though your own cards can still cause problems)

**What CLIP-LOCK does not do:**
- It does not protect against area-of-effect cards (like Centaur Problem, which affects all Fantasy Creations)
- It does not protect against your own Glitch tokens
- The Creation still generates Visibility and Reputation normally while locked

**One per turn, one at a time.** You can only lock one Creation per turn. You can lock a different Creation each turn if you like, or keep locking the same one (though it is already locked — re-locking does nothing).

**Important:** The turn you apply CLIP-LOCK does not count toward Positive Feedback's Loyalty gain. It starts counting from the *following* turn.

---

## AIA'S ABILITIES

**① OVERRENDER — Cost: 3 Reputation**

Target one opponent Creation. It loses 1 Quality. If that Creation already has 1 or more Glitch tokens, it loses 2 Quality instead. If its Quality reaches 0, it is destroyed immediately — and its Creator loses 1 Loyalty.

*This is your main removal tool. Use it to chip away at high-Quality opponent Creations, especially ones that already have Glitch tokens on them. It is also a finishing blow on already-damaged Creations.*

**② POSITIVE FEEDBACK — Cost: 5 Reputation**

Choose one of your CLIP-LOCKed Creations and remove its CLIP-LOCK. Gain 1 Loyalty for each full turn that Creation has been CLIP-LOCKed, up to a maximum of 3 Loyalty.

*Remember: the turn you applied CLIP-LOCK does not count. A Creation you locked last turn and are unlocking this turn has been locked for 1 full turn — you gain 1 Loyalty. One that has been locked for 3 or more full turns gains you the maximum 3 Loyalty.*

*Positive Feedback is both healing and setup. Use it when you need Loyalty back, or to clear a lock you no longer need.*

**③ IRIDESCENT SHIFT — Cost: 6 Reputation**

Target one of your active Creations. It gains 2 Visibility Counters and cannot be targeted by opponent single-target abilities until the start of your next turn.

*This is your tempo and protection ability. Push a Creation toward Featured, or protect a vulnerable one before your opponent's turn. It works on any active Creation, not just CLIP-LOCKed ones.*

**⚡ COPY THAT! — Cost: 4 Loyalty · 14 Reputation**

Choose up to 3 of your active CLIP-LOCKed Creations. Remove CLIP-LOCK from each. Each one deals 1 Loyalty damage to a target opponent Creator. If 2 or more of them target the same Creator, that Creator also loses 2 additional Reputation.

*This is the win condition. Three locked Creations hitting the same Creator = 3 Loyalty damage + 2 Reputation loss. At 14 Reputation this is expensive, but you will have been accumulating Reputation safely behind your locks all game. The 4 Loyalty cost hurts — only fire when you know it finishes the job or comes close to it.*

## GUARANTEED MODELS

Aia's deck includes two guaranteed Model cards set aside before play:

**COHERENT (LOW SETTINGS)** — Quality 3, Runtime 2. Compatible: Fantasy, Landscape. Incompatible: Portrait. Creations generated by Coherent enter with +1 bonus Visibility Counter. CLIP-LOCK eligible.

**JUGGERNAUT V9** — Quality 2, Runtime 2. Compatible: Portrait, Abstract. Incompatible: Landscape. Abstract Creations generated by Juggernaut gain +1 Quality on entry.

*Coherent is your main engine. Juggernaut is your Turn 1 fallback when going first, and a secondary generator for Abstract Creations.*

---

## THE CLIP-LOCK ENGINE — HOW THE DECK FLOWS

Here is how a typical Aia game develops:

**Early turns (1–4):** Place Models, generate your first Coherent Creation. Keep it safe. Apply CLIP-LOCK as soon as it enters the field. Consider Juggernaut for an early Abstract Creation to build Reputation faster.

**Mid game (5–10):** Build up 2–3 CLIP-LOCKed Creations. Use Overrender to pick off opponent Creations that threaten you. Use Positive Feedback occasionally to gain back Loyalty and clear old locks. Let your Reputation build behind your locks — they are generating safely and your opponent cannot touch them.

**Late game:** When you have 2–3 locked Creations, enough Reputation, and your opponent is sufficiently weakened, fire Copy That! One burst of coordinated damage, unlocking everything at once.

## KEY CARDS IN THE AIA DECK

**ARE YOU CRAZY?! (Prompt — Style)** — Gives the generated Creation +3 Quality but increases Runtime by 1. Only compatible with Coherent variants and SD1.5. *Use with Coherent for a Quality 6 Creation. Devastating behind CLIP-LOCK.*

**COPYCAT... I MEAN, COPYGAZELLE... (Prompt — Negative)** — The generated Creation cannot be affected by opponent Modifiers, Artifacts, abilities or Glitch tokens for 3 turns. *Even stronger protection than CLIP-LOCK in the short term. Stack with CLIP-LOCK for nearly untouchable Creations.*

**WHAT'S WRONG WITH THE HANDS? (Prompt — Negative)** — +1 Quality. Simple and efficient for a 1 Credit cost.

**PRIORITY RENDERING (Event — Instant)** — Move one queued Creation to arrive at the start of your next turn. *Use this when Coherent's Runtime 2 is too slow and you need your Creation earlier.*

**GPU BOOST (Event — Instant)** — Reduce one queued Creation's Runtime by 2. *Similar use to Priority Rendering — accelerates Coherent.*

**QUEUE SKIP (Modifier)** — Attach to any Model. The next Creation it generates arrives immediately, bypassing Runtime entirely. *Powerful on Coherent — a Quality 3 Creation that skips the wait entirely.*

**PRO SUBSCRIPTION (Modifier)** — Activate one Model at half cost once per turn. +3 Credit cap. +1 Credit at start of each turn. −1 Runtime on all your Creations. *Use this to sustain long games and churn out Coherent Creations faster.*

## DECKBUILDING NOTE

Both the Coherent Model and Juggernaut are guaranteed — they are in every game. The shuffled deck (37 cards) is built around supporting them: quality boosts, queue acceleration, Glitch removal, and the Copy That! setup chain.

Unlike Anonymous User who wants the field flooded, Aia wants the field controlled. You will rarely have more than 2–3 active Creations — but each one will be better protected and more powerful than anything across the table.

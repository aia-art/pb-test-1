# FLAGGED ITEMS FOR BETA TESTING
## Prompt Battle TCG · Set 001: First Render · v0.14

---

*This document lists the specific things we need your eyes on during beta. For each item, there is a short description of what we are testing, what the concern is, and what kind of feedback is most useful. Use the Feedback button or the Suggest/Vote features to send your thoughts in.*

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Work through the list as you play. You do not need to test every item in every game — just note when something comes up naturally and report what happened. The more specific you can be (which cards were in play, what turn it was, what felt wrong), the more useful your feedback.

Priority items are marked **⚑ HIGH PRIORITY**.

---

## ITEM 1 — SD 1.5 ENTRY DESTRUCTION ⚑ HIGH PRIORITY

**What it is:** Stable Diffusion 1.5 always gives all its Creations +1 Glitch token on entry. At base Quality 1, any Creation generated without a Quality boost enters at effective Quality 0 and is destroyed immediately — before it ever reaches the field.

**The concern:** Is this interaction intuitive? Does it feel punishing in a satisfying way (high risk, high reward) or just confusing and frustrating? Does the card text make it clear enough that this will happen?

**What to look for:**
- When you first play SD1.5, did you understand that you needed a Quality-boosting Prompt to keep the Creation alive?
- Did it feel like a meaningful deckbuilding challenge, or like a trap for newer players?
- Does SD1.5 see play? Or does it get cut from custom decks immediately?

**The vote:** There is a design vote open for this in the Cards → Votes section. Please cast your vote there.

---

## ITEM 2 — CLIP-LOCK ENGAGEMENT ⚑ HIGH PRIORITY

**What it is:** Aia's passive lets her lock one Coherent Creation per turn. A locked Creation is protected from opponent single-target effects and Glitch tokens. Positive Feedback rewards keeping a Creation locked over multiple turns.

**The concern:** Is CLIP-LOCK fun to use? Does the "lock and wait" gameplay feel strategic and rewarding, or tedious and passive? Does it feel satisfying when you finally release the lock, or anticlimactic?

**What to look for:**
- Do you find yourself applying CLIP-LOCK every turn, or only situationally?
- Does Positive Feedback (the unlock ability) feel worth the setup?
- Does your opponent feel helpless against a locked Creation, or do they have enough ways to work around it?
- Does CLIP-LOCK encourage interesting decisions, or does it basically always do the same thing?

**The vote:** There is a design vote open for CLIP-LOCK application limits in the Cards → Votes section.

---

## ITEM 3 — REPUTATION ECONOMY AND THE 20-CAP

**What it is:** Reputation is capped at 20 and is used to pay for Creator abilities. It is earned passively from Creations based on their Visibility.

**The concern:** Does 20 feel like the right cap? Is it too easy to reach 20 and constantly waste Reputation? Or is it hard to accumulate enough to use abilities frequently?

**What to look for:**
- How often are you hitting the 20 Reputation cap?
- How often are you unable to use abilities because you do not have enough Reputation?
- Does the Reputation economy feel balanced between the two decks?
- Do you feel like you are making interesting decisions about when to spend Reputation, or does it feel like an afterthought?

---

## ITEM 4 — GOING VIRAL TIMING AND POWER LEVEL

**What it is:** Anonymous User's Signature ability. Costs 4 Loyalty and 12 Reputation. All active Creations gain 3 Visibility — each one that crosses a threshold deals 1 Loyalty damage to the target Creator.

**The concern:** Is Going Viral a satisfying climax to the Horde strategy, or is it too hard to set up? Too easy? Does the Loyalty cost feel like a meaningful sacrifice, or is it trivially outweighed by the damage dealt?

**What to look for:**
- How many games end with Going Viral going off?
- When it fires, does it feel decisive and earned?
- If you never used it, why not? Was it too expensive, too situational, or too easy to play around?
- Is 12 Reputation the right cost? Too high? Too low?

---

## ITEM 5 — DRAW RATE

**What it is:** Players draw 1 card at the end of each turn.

**The concern:** Is 1 card per turn enough? Do players run out of things to do? Does hand size feel restrictive or comfortable?

**What to look for:**
- Do you often end turns with an empty hand?
- Do you find yourself holding cards you cannot afford to play?
- Would 2 cards per turn feel better, or would it make the game too explosive?

---

## ITEM 6 — MASS REPORT CLARITY

**What it is:** E-001 (Mass Report) is the only card that can be played during the opponent's turn — but only in response to a Modifier being played, and it cannot cancel The Astronaut or PRO Subscription.

**The concern:** Is the exception (cannot cancel those two Modifiers) clear enough on the card? Is the "play during opponent's turn" mechanic intuitive, or does it cause confusion?

**What to look for:**
- Did you find the card text clear about when it can and cannot be used?
- Did any disputes come up about it at the table?
- Does the inability to cancel The Astronaut feel fair and reasonable, or frustrating?

---

## ITEM 7 — THE ASTRONAUT POWER LEVEL

**What it is:** MO-001 (The Astronaut) is the rarest Modifier in the set (Mythic). It cannot be cancelled, gives +3 Loyalty on attach, boosts all Creations generated while active, and adds 1 Visibility Counter to all active Creations each turn. On detach, it applies 1 Glitch token to each active Creation.

**The concern:** Is The Astronaut appropriately powerful for its cost and rarity? Is the detach penalty scary enough to counterbalance the upside?

**What to look for:**
- Does The Astronaut feel game-deciding when it hits the table?
- Is the detach penalty (Glitch tokens on all your Creations) a serious risk, or trivially managed?
- Does the uncancellable nature feel frustrating to play against, or accepted given its visibility as a Mythic card?

---

## ITEM 8 — CONTENTION IN PRACTICE

**What it is:** When a Model is activated for the second time in the same round (by either player), the second Creation generated gets +1 Runtime.

**The concern:** Does Contention come up naturally? Is it noticeable when it happens? Does it feel like a meaningful mechanic or an afterthought?

**What to look for:**
- Did Contention actually affect your game?
- Was it clear when it applied?
- Did it influence which Models you chose to activate, or when?

---

## ITEM 9 — CREATOR STRESS FEEL

**What it is:** If you end your turn with no Creations on the field and nothing in your Queue, your Creator loses 1 Loyalty.

**The concern:** Does Creator Stress feel like a natural pressure mechanic that keeps the game moving, or does it feel like an unfair punishment?

**What to look for:**
- Did Creator Stress come up in your games?
- When it triggered, did it feel fair and telegraphed?
- Are there situations where it feels impossible to avoid?

---

## ITEM 10 — DAILY CHALLENGES RELEVANCE

**What it is:** E-009 and E-010 (Daily Challenge: Abstractions and Daily Challenge: Portraits) are Main Phase Events that reward the player with the most Creations of a specific Style at the end of the round.

**The concern:** Are the Daily Challenges worth including in decks? Do they come up naturally, or do they sit in your hand because the conditions are too hard to meet?

**What to look for:**
- Did you play a Daily Challenge in any of your games?
- Was the bonus meaningful when it triggered?
- Did it feel like it influenced your deckbuilding or in-game decisions?

---

## GENERAL FEEDBACK

Beyond specific items, these are the broad questions we are most interested in:

- **First impression:** How did the game feel the first time you played it? What was confusing? What clicked immediately?
- **Pacing:** Do games feel the right length? Too long, too short?
- **Balance:** Did one deck feel consistently stronger? Were there turns where one player felt unable to do anything?
- **Vocabulary:** Were there any terms or concepts that took too long to understand? Anything that should be explained differently?
- **Fun:** What was the most fun moment in your games? What was the least fun?

All of this is useful. Do not hold back.

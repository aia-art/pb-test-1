## THE STRUCTURE OF A LEGAL DECK

Every Quick Duel deck is exactly **fourty cards**, split into three parts:

| Part | Count | Goes in deck? |
|---|---|---|
| Creator card | 1 | No — sits in front of you |
| Guaranteed Model cards | 2 | No — set aside before play |
| Shuffled deck | 37 | Yes |

Your Creator and guaranteed Models are visible from the start of the game — they are not hidden information. The 37-card shuffled deck is what you draw from.

---

## CARD LIMITS

- **Maximum 3 copies** of any single card in the shuffled deck
- **The Astronaut (MO-001): maximum 1 copy** across the entire deck — it is the only Set 001 card with a deck limit below 3
- Creator cards cannot go in the shuffled deck
- Cards designated as another deck's guaranteed Models cannot go in your shuffled deck (you are free to include non-guaranteed copies of a Model, though — for example, including SDXL in an Aia deck)

## CHOOSING YOUR CREATOR

Your Creator defines your playstyle more than any other card. In Set 001 you have two options:

**AIA** (Loyalty 11 · Starter Bonus: +1 Credit)
Patient and precise. Aia is built around generating a small number of high-Quality, protected Creations and using them as weapons. Her CLIP-LOCK mechanic makes her Creations nearly untouchable during the build-up, and Copy That! can close out the game suddenly. Aia punishes opponents who spread damage around inefficiently — but she cannot absorb much punishment herself.

*Best for players who enjoy planning several turns ahead and executing a strategy precisely.*

**ANONYMOUS USER** (Loyalty 16 · Starter Bonus: +1 Reputation)
Fast and overwhelming. Anon generates Creations quickly and cheaply, floods the field, and uses sheer volume and Visibility pressure to overwhelm opponents. Going Viral rewards you for having many Creations near thresholds at once — a feat Anon achieves more easily than any other Creator.

*Best for players who enjoy aggressive, board-flooding strategies and want flexibility in how they apply pressure.*

## GUARANTEED MODELS — YOUR FOUNDATION

Your two guaranteed Models are set aside before the shuffled deck and are in every game. They anchor your strategy.

**AIA'S GUARANTEED MODELS:**
- Coherent (Low Settings) — Quality 3, Runtime 2. CLIP-LOCK eligible. Your primary engine.
- Juggernaut v9 — Quality 2, Runtime 2. Abstract bonus. Turn 1 fallback.

**ANONYMOUS USER'S GUARANTEED MODELS:**
- SDXL — Quality 2, Runtime 1. Portrait/Landscape compatible. Fast generator.
- Stable Diffusion 1.5 — Quality 1, Runtime 1. Free to play and activate. Always enters with +1 Glitch token — requires Quality support to survive.

You can include additional copies of Models in your shuffled deck if you want more access to them or want to use them as redundancy.

## BUILDING THE 37-CARD SHUFFLED DECK

Think of your shuffled deck as four layers:

### LAYER 1: QUALITY AND CREATION SUPPORT (10–13 cards)

These are your Prompt cards and any cards that affect the Quality, Visibility or Glitch token count of your Creations on entry. They make your Creations better.

For Aia: prioritise Quality boosts (Are You Crazy?!, What's Wrong with the Hands?) and protection Prompts (Copycat... I Mean, Copygazelle...).

For Anon: prioritise Visibility bonuses on entry (Men..., Did You Steal This Prompt?) and Style tags that support her Passive and Daily Challenge events.

### LAYER 2: QUEUE MANAGEMENT (6–9 cards)

These are cards that manipulate Runtime — accelerating your Creations to the field faster or slowing down opponents' Creations.

Aia: Priority Rendering, GPU Boost, Queue Skip.
Anon: Queue Crash (delays opponents), Flood the Feed support.

### LAYER 3: CONTROL AND DISRUPTION (6–9 cards)

Cards that interact with opponent Creations, Models or Creators. Modifiers, targeted Events, and Artifacts that shift the board in your favour.

Aia: Centaur Problem (punishes Fantasy-heavy opponents), Algorithm Swap, Generation Cancelled.
Anon: Queue Crash, Community Drama, Ban.

### LAYER 4: RESOURCE AND SUSTAINABILITY (8–12 cards)

Cards that keep your engine running — Credit income, Modifier buffs, and cards that generate value over multiple turns.

Aia: PRO Subscription (key resource enabler), Trending, Tip Received.
Anon: Credit Drop, Server Overload (if you can weather it), Daily Challenges.

## MANA CURVE — KEEPING THE COSTS BALANCED

Roughly aim for this distribution across the 37-card shuffled deck:

| Credit cost | Suggested count |
|---|---|
| 0 | 2–3 |
| 1 | 7–10 |
| 2 | 9–13 |
| 3 | 6–9 |
| 4+ | 2–4 |

Having too many expensive cards means slow starts. Having too many free or cheap cards means you run out of impact in the late game. Find the balance for your strategy.

## WHAT MAKES A GOOD CUSTOM DECK?

**A clear win condition.** Know how you intend to win — not just "reduce their Loyalty to 0" but specifically which cards and sequences get you there. Build the rest of the deck to support that plan.

**Multiple routes to the same outcome.** If your win condition is blocked, you need a backup. Two or three overlapping strategies are better than one that can be shut down by a single card.

**A consistent early game.** The first 3 turns are critical. Make sure you can do something meaningful every turn from Turn 1 — avoid hands where you cannot afford to play anything relevant until Turn 4.

**Respect the Credit curve.** Your deck generates 5 Credits per turn. On an average turn, you might spend 3–5 Credits. Build your deck around what you can realistically afford.

**Leave room for adaptation.** Not every card you draw will be useful in every game. Cards that are flexible — useful in multiple situations — are more consistent than highly situational ones.
